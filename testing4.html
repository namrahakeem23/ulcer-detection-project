📋 About the Project

UlcerDetect AI is an innovative deep learning-based system designed to assist healthcare professionals in the early detection and analysis of skin ulcers. Leveraging a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network trained on over 50,000 medical images, our solution provides rapid, non-invasive, and highly accurate ulcer detection. The platform aims to reduce diagnostic time, improve patient outcomes, and enable remote wound assessment, making quality healthcare more accessible worldwide.
